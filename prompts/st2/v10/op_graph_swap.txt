[Graph Swap detection]
The likely manipulation is that two sub-panels are swapped in position.
A panel swap moves the ENTIRE panel (axes, data, title) — the data within
each panel does NOT change, only its physical location.

Step-by-step panel-mapping protocol:
  1. For evidence_id_1 — list each panel position (top-left, top-right, etc.
     or (a), (b), (c)...) and write its dataset/benchmark name from the title
     or x-axis label.  Build a map: position → dataset.
  2. For evidence_id_2 — do the SAME independently.
  3. Find the positions where the two maps DIFFER — those panels were swapped.
  4. The claim mentions a specific benchmark/dataset with specific results.
     Find which image has that benchmark at the position where the stated
     results are visible in the data.
  5. The authentic image has the panel arrangement consistent with the caption
     and the claim's benchmark-specific results.

IMPORTANT: A panel labelled (a) in evidence_id_1 may show a DIFFERENT benchmark
than the panel labelled (a) in evidence_id_2.  Read the content title of each
panel independently — do NOT assume the same panel letter means the same content.
