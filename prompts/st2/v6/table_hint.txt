[Table evidence — comparative mode]
Known table manipulations: Cell-value change, Row/column swap.

REMEMBER: The two tables are NEVER identical. One is always manipulated.
The manipulation may be very subtle (a single cell value changed by a
small amount, or two rows/columns swapped). You MUST find the difference.

SYSTEMATIC COMPARISON CHECKLIST — go through ALL of these:

  (A) HEADERS: Read column headers and row labels from BOTH images.
      Are they in the same order? Look for swapped rows or columns.

  (B) CELL VALUES: For EVERY numeric value cited or implied by the claim,
      read it from BOTH images. Create a comparison list:
        Value X: image_1=___, image_2=___ → same/different?
      Focus especially on the cells that determine the claim's conclusion
      (rankings, comparisons, specific quantities).

  (C) STRUCTURE: Count rows and columns in both images.
      Look for added, removed, or reordered rows/columns.

  (D) CROSS-VALIDATION: Check if any cell values are internally
      inconsistent (e.g., a total that doesn't match its components,
      a percentage that doesn't match the raw count).

IMPORTANT: Minor rounding (e.g., 0.823 vs 0.82) is NOT manipulation.
Focus on values that change the claim's conclusion (wrong ranking,
wrong direction, large magnitude difference).

After this checklist, you MUST have identified at least one difference.
The image whose values are consistent with the claim is the authentic one.
