[Graph Flip detection]
The likely manipulation is that one or more axes are reversed
(ascending ↔ descending direction).
  • Read the axis tick values at both ends in both images — is the axis
    increasing or decreasing from left-to-right (or bottom-to-top)?
  • The claim implies a specific trend direction; the authentic image has
    the axis orientation that makes that trend correct.
  • A flipped axis makes a decreasing trend appear increasing.
